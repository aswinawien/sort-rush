## Deferred proposals — analysed, not scheduled

These went further than a backlog note: both were worked through with options, evidence and a recommendation, and both live in `docs/decision-log.md` as `proposed`. They are parked here rather than duplicated, because nothing depends on them and the approved-but-unbuilt queue is already long.

- **Dynamic chutes in endless** — count progression and lane swapping recommended, morphing recommended against. Blocked in practice on a scope-ceiling ruling (bins are fixed at 3) and on the fact that levels 8–10 are already booked.
- **XP bar and roguelite upgrades** — the XP bar already exists as the pressure index `P`, so it is a presentation change rather than a system. Four proposed upgrades were recommended against for deciding outcomes on the player's behalf.
